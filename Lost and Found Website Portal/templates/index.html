How SmartRecover Works

From losing an item to safe verification and handover in four transparent steps.

STEP 01

Report an Item

Submit item details, date, campus location, photos, and confidential identifying markers.

STEP 02

Find Smart Matches

Our Python engine instantly compares reports within your college across 5 weighted factors.

STEP 03

Verify Ownership

Claimants submit private proof. Finders and administrators review claims securely without data leaks.

STEP 04

Recover Belongings

Coordinate return at designated security points and close reports with confirmed status updates.

Key Architectural Pillars

Why Choose SmartRecover

Designed specifically for multi-college networks with complete institutional boundary isolation.

Intelligent Weighted Matching

Combines title sequence analysis (30%), category taxonomy (20%), descriptive semantics (25%), location tokens (15%), and chronological proximity (10%).

Secure Ownership Verification

Private serial numbers, wallpapers, and identifiable marks are protected from the public. Only reviewers examine claim proof.

Multi-Institution Boundaries

Strict logical partitioning ensures student items, matches, and administration stay cleanly segregated within each college.

End-to-End Status Tracking

Interactive lifecycle timeline guides each report from initial post through smart match, claim review, and physical handover.
